# safe-driving-system


## Overview
The **Safe Driving System** is an innovative graduation project designed to enhance road safety by monitoring drivers’ health conditions in real time. The system addresses a critical risk factor in driving accidents: sudden health issues that may impair a driver’s ability to operate a vehicle safely.

By combining **wearable health technology**, **wireless connectivity**, and a **mobile application**, the system provides continuous health monitoring, early detection of abnormal conditions, and timely alerts to assist drivers when needed.

---

## Problem Statement
Drivers may experience unexpected health complications such as abnormal heart rate or blood pressure fluctuations while driving. These conditions can significantly increase the risk of accidents if not detected early.

Traditional vehicles lack integrated systems capable of monitoring the driver's health status and responding intelligently to such situations.

---

## System Objectives
- Monitor vital health signs of the driver in real time  
- Detect abnormal physiological changes that may affect driving safety  
- Alert the driver immediately when critical conditions are detected  
- Enhance road safety and reduce accident risks caused by health issues  

---

## System Architecture
The Safe Driving System consists of **two main components**:

### 1. Mobile Application
- Acts as the central control and monitoring hub  
- Displays real-time health data in a user-friendly interface  
- Communicates with the wearable device via **Wi-Fi connection through the vehicle**  
- Sends alerts and notifications when abnormal health conditions are detected  

### 2. Wearable Health Device
- Worn by the driver during driving  
- Equipped with sensors to monitor vital signs such as:
  - Heart rate  
  - Blood pressure  
  - Other relevant physiological parameters  
- Continuously transmits health data to the mobile application  

---

## How the System Works
1. The wearable device continuously monitors the driver’s vital signs  
2. Health data is transmitted wirelessly to the mobile application  
3. The application analyzes incoming data in real time  
4. If abnormal values are detected:
   - The driver is alerted immediately  
   - The system suggests appropriate actions (rest, stop driving, seek medical help, etc.)
